## Audience signal

Create one audience signal named `US English Creators - Intent`.

Custom search terms:

- video script generator
- AI script writer
- teleprompter app
- video transcript app
- TikTok transcript
- YouTube transcript
- Instagram Reels ideas
- content creator tools
- social media content planner
- hook generator
- caption generator
- repurpose content

Related apps and sites can include CapCut, Canva, Descript, Notion, Captions and established teleprompter apps. Keep demographics broad; an audience signal guides learning and should not be treated as strict targeting.

If eligible lists contain at least 1,000 active users, also attach Android app-user, Firebase or website-visitor lists. Do not upload a small customer list merely to fill the field.

## Bidding and budget

For the initial campaign:

- Focus: `Install volume`
- Install tracking: Google Play download conversion
- Users: `All users`
- Target CPI: start near Google's current in-product suggestion, `SGD 1.19`, only if this is economically acceptable
- Recommended daily budget at that tCPI: at least `SGD 60` (about 50 times tCPI)

If SGD 60/day is not acceptable, decide the maximum monthly test spend first instead of entering an artificially low tCPI. A constrained tCPI can prevent delivery. Do not launch a second United States install campaign with the same goal because the campaigns can compete.

## Measurement and iteration

Track installs plus meaningful funnel events such as first video import, successful transcript, first script generated, teleprompter opened, trial started and subscription purchased. Start by optimizing for installs; only create a separate in-app-action campaign after the chosen action has enough steady volume.

After launch, allow 7-14 days for learning. Avoid changing budget or tCPI by more than 20% at once. Review asset labels after learning and replace `Low` assets gradually with new variants inspired by `Best` assets.
