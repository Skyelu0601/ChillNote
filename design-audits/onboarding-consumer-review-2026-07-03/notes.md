# ChillNote Onboarding Consumer Review

Date: 2026-07-03
Scope: First-run onboarding screenshots captured with `-OnboardingScreenshotMode` on iPhone 17 simulator.

## Steps

1. Hero
   - Screenshot: `01-hero.jpg`
   - Health: Good visual trust, medium conversion clarity.
   - Note: The brand and icon orbit feel polished. The promise is still broad: "Save videos, extract ideas, and create content with AI" says category value, but not a specific result a creator can imagine using today.

2. Share Extension Demo
   - Screenshot: `02-share-extension.jpg`
   - Health: Strong real-world context, medium friction.
   - Note: The TikTok-style example makes the product feel concrete. Requiring two play-through steps before continuing teaches the workflow, but it also turns onboarding into a task before the user has felt enough payoff.

3. Capture Showcase
   - Screenshot: `03-capture.jpg`
   - Health: Strongest page for value.
   - Note: This page shows the clearest transformation: messy voice idea to usable creator note. The card has a lot of text, so the page depends on quick scanning of title, source, and note heading rather than full reading.

4. AI Skills
   - Screenshot: `04-ai-skills.jpg`
   - Health: Strong product positioning, incomplete proof.
   - Note: The skills list communicates that ChillNote is not just storage or chat. It would be more persuasive if one skill showed a visible before/after output or a concrete generated caption/hook result.

## Consumer Verdict

I would continue and try the product. I would not be fully ready to pay from onboarding alone.

The flow sells the category and product shape well, especially for creators who save TikTok or YouTube inspiration. The missing piece is a stronger "aha" before the trial ask: show one finished output that makes the user think, "I could post or use that immediately."

## Highest-impact Recommendations

1. Make page 3 or 4 show a finished output, not only captured notes or skill names.
2. Consider making the share-extension video skippable after the first tap, or unlock Next while the video is playing.
3. Strengthen the first-page promise with a more concrete creator outcome.
4. On the trial page, connect the price ask directly to the concrete output the user just saw.

## Evidence Limits

The onboarding screenshots were captured successfully. The `Get started` button in screenshot mode did not proceed into the actual post-onboarding paywall, so paywall comments are based on the current `SubscriptionView(context: .onboardingTrial)` implementation and localization keys, not a fresh screenshot of that page.
