### Full description（少于 4000 字符）

```text
Capture inspiration before it disappears. ChillScript is an AI creator notes and script-writing app that turns saved videos, voice notes and rough ideas into hooks, captions and scripts.

SAVE CREATOR INSPIRATION

Share or paste a creator link from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram Reels and other supported sites. ChillScript keeps the source with your note and can extract useful details and a transcript when available.

CAPTURE IDEAS YOUR WAY

• Record a voice note and turn it into editable text
• Type a quick thought before you lose it
• Save creator links from Android’s Share menu
• Use the home-screen widget or Quick Settings tile for faster voice capture

TURN NOTES INTO CONTENT

Use creator-focused AI Skills to:

• Generate scroll-stopping hooks
• Rewrite text in a more natural, human voice
• Create captions and platform-ready variations
• Turn an idea into a timed video script
• Repurpose one note into several content formats
• Translate or summarize selected text
• Build your own reusable AI Skill

CREATE FROM YOUR OWN LIBRARY

Select multiple notes and ask AI to combine what you have already captured. Save a useful AI response as a new note and keep developing it without starting from a blank page.

STAY ORGANIZED

Move work through Inbox, Drafts and Published. Add nested tags, pin important notes, search your full library and recover deleted notes from Trash. Your notes are stored locally for offline access and sync with your account when you are online.

RECORD WITH A TELEPROMPTER

Open a finished script in the built-in teleprompter, adjust the text size and scroll speed, record multiple clips, retake a section and export the finished video.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR WORK

Preview AI changes before applying them. Undo a result, retry it with new instructions or save it as a separate note. Export one note or download your complete notes library as a ZIP file.

ChillScript is built for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, podcasts, newsletters and any creator who wants a calmer path from inspiration to a finished script.

Download ChillScript and turn the ideas you save today into the content you publish next.

Privacy Policy: https://www.chillnoteai.com/privacy
Terms of Use: https://www.chillnoteai.com/terms
Account deletion: https://www.chillnoteai.com/delete-account
```

## Play Console 建议设置

- 应用类别：`Productivity`
- 标签：`Note-taking`、`Voice recorder`、`Writing`、`Video editing`（以 Play Console 实际可选标签为准）
- 是否含广告：`No`
- 主要目标用户：成年内容创作者；产品并非专门面向儿童
- 联系邮箱：使用正式支持邮箱，不要填写私人邮箱
- 隐私政策：`https://www.chillnoteai.com/privacy`
- 账号删除网址：`https://www.chillnoteai.com/delete-account`
